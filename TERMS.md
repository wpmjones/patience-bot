# Terms and Conditions — Patience Bot

**Last updated:** August 27, 2026

## What this covers

Patience Bot is a moderation app for Reddit, operated by the moderator team of
[r/ClashOfClansRecruit](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClashOfClansRecruit) and built
on Reddit's Developer Platform.

These terms apply to the moderators who install it and to the people who post in
a subreddit where it is running. Using such a subreddit means these terms apply
to you.

## What the app does

Patience Bot reads posts as they are submitted and applies the subreddit's
posting rules. It:

- checks that the title starts with a permitted category tag and follows the
  format the subreddit requires
- looks up the clan tag in the title against the Clash of Clans API to confirm
  the clan exists and that its name appears in the title
- enforces the limit of one post per clan, and one post per author, per week
- removes posts that break these rules, and explains why in a comment on the
  post and in a private message from the subreddit
- flags posts mentioning buying, selling, trading, gifting, giveaways or gems
  for a moderator to look at, without removing them
- notifies moderators in a private Discord channel

## Moderators decide, not the app

Patience Bot is a tool the moderators use. It does not replace them.

Every removal it makes can be reviewed and reversed by a moderator, and
moderators may remove posts the bot allowed. Decisions about what is permitted
on a subreddit belong to that subreddit's moderator team, and questions about a
removal should go to them.

Being told your post broke a rule is not an accusation of bad faith. Most
removals are formatting mistakes.

## If you post in a subreddit where it runs

- Follow the subreddit's posting rules. They are in the sidebar and take
  precedence over anything the bot's messages say.
- Do not try to evade the bot's checks — for example by deliberately misspelling
  a clan tag, posting under another account to get around the weekly limit, or
  reposting removed content unchanged. Moderators may act on this under the
  subreddit's own rules and Reddit's.
- The bot may contact you by comment and private message about your own posts.
  Replies to those messages reach the moderator team.

## If you install it as a moderator

- You are responsible for how it is configured on your subreddit and for the
  removals it makes there.
- You need your own Clash of Clans API token and your own Discord webhook. Keep
  them confidential. They are entered as app settings and are not shared with
  the authors of this app.
- The rule text the bot sends to authors names r/ClashOfClansRecruit and its
  rules. Adapt it before running the app on a different subreddit.
